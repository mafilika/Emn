Frequently Asked Questions

What is ducted air conditioning?

Ducted systems distribute cooled air from a central unit through ducts to several rooms or zones, giving even temperatures with hidden equipment.

Can you fix noisy or unbalanced ducting?

Yes. We inspect, rebalance and modify ducting to improve airflow and cut noise.
