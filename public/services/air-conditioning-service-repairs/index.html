Frequently Asked Questions

How often should my aircon be serviced?

At least once a year, ideally before summer. Commercial and high-use units benefit from a service every 3 to 6 months.

Why is my aircon not cooling?

Common causes are dirty filters, low refrigerant, a faulty fan or capacitor, or a blocked drain. A technician can diagnose it quickly.
